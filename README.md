### 🌟 What is Codie?
Codie is a digital marketplace for ready-made, high-quality templates for **mobile** and **web applications**. It helps:
- Developers earn **passive income** by selling clean, reusable templates.
- Tech companies and startups **save time, reduce costs**, and **build faster** without starting from scratch.

---

### 💼 Who is Codie for?
- 💻 **Freelancers & student developers**: Turn your projects into digital products.
- 🏢 **Startups & software companies**: Buy customizable templates instead of building from zero.

---

### 🔥 Why Codie?
✅ High-quality architecture (Flutter, React, Node.js, etc.)  
✅ Templates are organized into small reusable components  
✅ Support for UI/UX redesign and customization  
✅ Commission-based sales model for developers  
✅ Affiliate system & freemium community challenges

---

### 🧰 Tech Stack (used in templates)
- **Flutter / React / Next.js** for Frontend
- **Node.js / Firebase** for Backend
- **Figma** for UI/UX
